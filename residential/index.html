Upfront Pricing

Straight Answers and Upfront Prices

Effective HVAC service starts with knowing exactly what you're dealing with. Memphis HVAC provides honest, upfront pricing across Memphis, handling everything from a worn capacitor to a full system replacement. Every job begins with a thorough assessment of your equipment, including its age, tonnage, fuel type, refrigerant charge, and duct condition, followed by a clear recommendation and a written price before any work starts.

The trouble with HVAC failures is that many of them develop out of sight. A pinhole leak in the evaporator coil bleeds off refrigerant, the blower bearings wear down a little at a time, and a weak capacitor holds on until the first hot afternoon, when the compressor won't start. If your bills are climbing, airflow drops, or ice forms on the lines, have the system checked before a repair turns into a replacement.

We also help homeowners plan ahead. Most air conditioners and furnaces last 12 to 18 years, and a planned replacement is nearly always cheaper and less stressful than an emergency swap during a heat wave or cold snap. Our technicians know the climate demands common to Memphis and what they do to equipment over time, so we can tell you honestly whether your system has years left or is living on borrowed time.

Seasonal Tune-Ups

Tune-Ups That Keep Your System Running

The best repair is the one you never need. That's the whole point of our seasonal plans. On each scheduled visit, we clean coils and burners, confirm the refrigerant charge, test the electrical components, inspect the heat exchanger, flush the condensate drain, and swap in a new filter, all tailored to the equipment in your home.

No two maintenance plans look exactly alike. An older furnace needs a closer look than a brand-new heat pump, and a dusty area means condenser coils clog faster. We build spring cooling and fall heating visits around your system, your climate, and how your family uses it, so every tune-up helps keep the system efficient and extends the life of the equipment you've already paid for.

Homeowners are often surprised how much a regular maintenance schedule cuts operating costs. A clean coil transfers heat far more efficiently, a properly charged system stops overworking the compressor, and a fresh filter protects the blower motor. Most families find that a maintenance plan costs far less than replacing an HVAC system even one year early.

Sized for Your Home

Equipment Sized for Your Actual House

Proper sizing matters more than most homeowners realize. Before quoting new equipment, Memphis HVAC measures the details that drive your home's heating and cooling load: square footage, insulation, window exposure, and duct layout. That gives you a system that runs in long, efficient cycles, controls humidity, and holds every room at the temperature you set.

We walk you through the options for most homes, especially growing families with rising comfort demands. A single-stage system that served a small household rarely satisfies a finished basement and two added bedrooms, and a modern two-stage or variable-speed heat pump can hold tighter temperatures while cutting energy use significantly. If you're weighing furnace-and-AC against a heat pump, or ducted against ductless, our technician will lay out the honest trade-offs: SEER2 efficiency ratings, operating cost, electrical and venting requirements, and upfront installation work, so you can pick what fits your home and budget.

Common Problems

Common HVAC Problems We Fix in Memphis

Warm air from the vents, a furnace that won't ignite, ice on the indoor coil, leaking refrigerant: every one of these problems has a different cause and calls for its own repair. Memphis HVAC tracks down the exact fault before replacing anything, whether it's a condenser that hums without starting, a furnace that short-cycles, a thermostat that loses its settings, a clogged condensate drain, or a mini-split throwing error codes.

Some problems are mostly a nuisance, while others pose real risks. A weak capacitor simply keeps the compressor from starting, but a cracked heat exchanger can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling. We match the fix to the failure: capacitors, contactors, and fan motors on cooling systems, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards on furnaces, sealed and repaired ductwork where conditioned air is bleeding into the attic, and a proper refrigerant recharge after a repaired leak. Whatever you're facing, our team has the parts and experience to handle it.
